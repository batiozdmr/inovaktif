INFORMATION ABOUT OUR COOKIE POLICY

What are Cookies?

 

Cookies are small text files that are stored to your device via your browser (eg. Chrome, Safari) when you visit relevant web sites.

Cookies are used for the purposes like remembering your user ID and password, loading the website faster and with less data consumption and remembering your device and they do not store personal information like name, surname or gender.

Cookies are created by the providers facilitating the web site you are visiting. This way the provider can tell when you are visiting the same web site with the same device again. In this prospect, cookies are like ID cards that indicate when a user is visiting the same web site on their device.

Most of the cookies are optional and when you’re visiting our web site for the first time, you should be prompted with a cookie pop-up that asks about your cookie preferences. You also can opt-in and out of optional cookies under our cookies tab. You also can choose to completely opt out of any optional cookies and clear any previously saved cookies via your browser.

Is it Possible to Prevent the Use of Cookies?

As we mention in detail below, you can prevent any and all cookies via your device and/or browser. But with doing so, you may also be preventing some cookies that are essential for our web site to work properly so you might have trouble viewing our web site properly.

When you are prompted about your cookie preferences upon your first visit, you can choose ‘allow only essential cookies’ option to allow the usage of essential cookies while opting out of any and all non-essential ones. Non-essential ones include cookies that make our web site more user friendly like, remember your user ID and passwords, remember your viewing language preferences and provide faster loading with less data consumption. Non-essential cookies also include cookies that personalise your advertisements.

How are we, as Inovaktif, are Using Cookies?

We are using cookies to remember your viewing preferences, collect anonymous information about our visitors (eg. where are they visiting from, how long they have visited certain pages) as well as techinical and performance cookies.

We are using cookies for analtical purposes; to determine your location, duration of your visits as well as which device you are using to visit us and where in our website you visited.

How are we, as Tanfer Group, are Using Third Party Cookies?

We are using Google Analytics, a web analysis service. Cookies of this service are used to analyse and report the visits we are getting. You can find more information about Google Analytics as well as how to opt out from it in the link below.

https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=tr#infocollect

Which Kind of Cookies We are Using?

Session Cookies: This cookies are kept for the duration of your visit, they are used for the essential purposes like remembering the choices you make during your visit and they collect no personal info. They are automatically deleted when you close all your browser tabs.

Technical Cookies: This cookies ensure that our website is working properly and detect the areas that do not.

Persistent Cookies: These cookies increase the functionality of our website and provide a faster service. They are created permanently on your first visit to the site and are used to remember your preferences when you access our website again.

Analytic Cookies: These cookies are used to collect and analyse usage and/or statistics of our website. They do not match user IP/Device adresses with the data and collect/store their datas anonymously.
