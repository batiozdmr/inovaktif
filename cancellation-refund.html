Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2024

1. General Information

This Cancellation and Refund Policy sets out the procedures and principles for cancelling and refunding digital services offered through inovaktif.com by Inovaktif Technology and Media Inc. ("SELLER"). This policy has been prepared in accordance with the Turkish Consumer Protection Law No. 6502 and the Distance Contracts Regulation.

2. Right of Withdrawal

The BUYER has the right to withdraw from the agreement within 14 (fourteen) days without giving any reason and without paying any penalty, before the performance of the service has begun.

  • The 14-day withdrawal period begins from the date the service agreement is established.
  • Withdrawal notice may be sent by email to info@inovaktif.com or in writing to the SELLER.
  • If the right of withdrawal is exercised, the amount paid by the BUYER will be refunded within 14 days.

3. Exceptions to the Right of Withdrawal

The right of withdrawal cannot be exercised in the following cases:

  • Digital content and services whose performance has begun with the BUYER's consent
  • Services prepared and personalized specifically for the BUYER
  • When the service has been fully performed and the BUYER has confirmed this
  • For monthly subscription services, when the relevant billing period has already started

4. Refund Process

  • Refund requests must be sent to info@inovaktif.com.
  • Refund requests must include the order number, BUYER details, and reason for refund.
  • Approved refunds are processed within 14 business days using the original payment method.
  • For credit card refunds, it may take 2–10 business days for the amount to appear in the account depending on the bank's processing time.
  • For bank transfer payments, refunds are made to the IBAN provided by the BUYER.

5. Service Cancellation

For monthly or periodic subscription services, the BUYER may cancel the service by providing written notice at least 15 days before the next billing period. Fees paid for the current period are non-refundable; the service continues until the end of that period.

7. Disputes

Consumer Arbitration Committees and Consumer Courts have jurisdiction over disputes related to cancellation and refund procedures. Applications may be made through the e-Government > Consumer Information System (TÜBİS) platform.
