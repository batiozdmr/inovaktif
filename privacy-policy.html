1. Purpose and scope

“Inovaktif”, “we” and “us” mean İnovaktif Medya Ticaret Limited Şirketi. This policy covers personal data processed through our website, contact form, online store and related customer communication. It is intended to meet the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and, where applicable, comparable requirements.

By using the site you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree, please do not submit personal data through the site.

2. Data we collect

  • Identity and contact data you send us: name, email, phone, company, billing address, tax office and tax or national ID when you request an invoice.
  • Order data: products, quantities, amounts, delivery address and the payment method you choose.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, pages viewed and cookie identifiers described in the Cookie Policy.
  • Messages you send through the contact form.

Card numbers are entered on the payment provider’s secure form. We do not store full card numbers or CVV codes on our servers.

3. Why we use it

  • To answer enquiries and perform a sales contract you request.
  • To deliver products, issue invoices and handle cancellation or refund requests.
  • To keep the site secure, measure aggregate traffic and remember language and cart preferences.
  • To meet tax, consumer-law and accounting duties.

4. Sharing

We share data only with parties who need it to provide the service: payment infrastructure (iyzico), cargo carriers, hosting and email providers, and public authorities when the law requires it. We do not sell personal data.

5. Retention and security

Order and invoice records are kept for the periods required by Turkish commercial and tax law. Contact messages are kept only as long as needed to handle the request. We use access controls and encrypted connections (HTTPS) appropriate to the data.

6. Your rights

Under KVKK Article 11 you may ask us to confirm whether we process your data, to correct or delete it where the law allows, to learn who it was shared with, and to object to a result that is produced solely by automated processing. Send requests to info@inovaktif.com. You may also complain to the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).

7. Children

The site and store are not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above will change when we do. Continued use of the site after an update means you have been informed of the new text.

Siteyi kullanarak bu politikayı okuduğunuzu kabul edersiniz. Kabul etmiyorsanız site üzerinden kişisel veri göndermeyin.

2. İşlenen veriler

  • Bize ilettiğiniz kimlik ve iletişim verileri: ad, e-posta, telefon, şirket unvanı, fatura adresi, fatura için vergi dairesi ve vergi veya T.C. kimlik numarası.

  • Teknik veriler: IP adresi, tarayıcı türü, görüntülenen sayfalar ve Çerez Politikası’nda açıklanan çerez tanımlayıcıları.
