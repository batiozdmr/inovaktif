Article 2 — Subject

This agreement governs the rights and obligations of the parties regarding the sale and performance of digital services ordered electronically by the BUYER through the inovaktif.com website, in accordance with the Turkish Consumer Protection Law No. 6502 and the Distance Contracts Regulation.

Article 3 — Service Information

Information regarding the basic characteristics, type, quantity, price, and payment method of the service subject to this agreement is provided on the product/service page reviewed by the BUYER before ordering and on the order summary screen. This information forms an integral part of the agreement.

Article 4 — Price and Payment

  • The service fee is displayed in Turkish Lira (TRY) on the order summary screen.
  • Prices exclude VAT; VAT is specified separately at the invoicing stage.
  • Payment may be made by credit card, debit card, or bank transfer.
  • For installment payments, transactions are processed within the agreement between the BUYER's bank and the payment provider.
  • The iyzico payment infrastructure is used for secure payments. Card details are not stored by the SELLER.

Article 5 — Performance of Service

  • Digital services begin within the period determined by the SELLER after payment confirmation.
  • Service start date and details are communicated to the BUYER via order confirmation email.
  • Monthly subscription services require a minimum contract period of 3 months.
  • The SELLER fulfills its obligations within the period specified in the agreement, except in cases of force majeure or impediments.

Article 6 — Right of Withdrawal

The BUYER has the right to withdraw from the agreement within 14 (fourteen) days without giving any reason and without paying any penalty, before the performance of the service has begun.

The right of withdrawal cannot be exercised in the following cases:

  • When performance of the service has begun with the BUYER's consent,
  • Services performed instantly in electronic form (e.g., digital reporting, access activation),
  • Services personalized at the BUYER's request and approval.

Withdrawal notice may be sent by email to info@inovaktif.com.

Article 7 — Privacy

The SELLER protects the BUYER's personal data under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and privacy policy; it is not shared with third parties. See our Cookie and Privacy Policy for details.

Article 8 — Dispute Resolution

Turkish courts and enforcement offices have jurisdiction over disputes arising from this agreement. The BUYER may submit complaints to Consumer Arbitration Committees or Consumer Courts. Applications may be made through the e-Government > Consumer Information System (TÜBİS) platform.

Article 9 — Effectiveness

By checking "I have read and accept the Distance Sales Agreement" at the order confirmation step, the BUYER declares that they have read, understood, and accepted all provisions of this agreement. The agreement enters into force upon order confirmation.
